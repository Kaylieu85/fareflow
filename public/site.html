FAQ

Good questions.

How do I download it on iPhone or Android?
FareFlow is a Progressive Web App — no app-store review wait. Open fareflow.link/get on your phone: on Android tap Install app; on iPhone tap Share → Add to Home Screen. It installs like a native app with its own icon, splash screen and push-speed updates.
How do I sign in?
With your email or mobile number, plus your password — your choice, same account. First time here? Tap Create an account: it sets up your driver profile automatically.
How does FareFlow know which app accounts are mine?
Each company issues you a driver number (your Uber ID, Bolt ID, and so on). In Settings → App connections you link each app with the number it gave you — FareFlow then routes that app's jobs only to drivers linked on it. Got multiple drivers? Each links their own numbers.
Is FareFlow made by Uber, Bolt or the other apps?
No — FareFlow is an independent channel manager, the same way hotel tools sit on top of Booking.com and Expedia. It works alongside the operator apps; we're not affiliated with or endorsed by Uber, Bolt, FREE NOW, Gett, Veezu or Addison Lee.
What happens when two apps offer me the same time?
That's the whole point. Accept the one you want — the slot plus your buffer is blocked on every other app in about a second, and any clashing offer that still arrives is politely auto-declined to protect your acceptance rating.
Do my riders need to install anything?
Never. They get a text with a tracking link that opens in any browser — live ETA, your vehicle and reg, and their 4-digit pickup code. When you enter the code at pickup it verifies both of you.
Can my dispatch system plug in directly?
Yes — each channel has an authenticated webhook (per-channel API keys, offer injection, cancellations and a jobs feed). Micro-cab offices and fleet PAMM systems can push offers straight into the router.
How much does it cost?
While we're in early access it's free for owner-drivers. Fleet plans with multi-driver routing and the operator API are priced per seat — join the list and we'll text you when seats open.
